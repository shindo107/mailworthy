# Friends and Bindings

Have a guildmate who enchants? A friend levelling tailoring? A **binding** sends a profession's materials to them every time you mail, without you thinking about it.

*"Send my Enchanting materials to Bob."* That's one binding.

## Making one

1. Open **Friends...**: in [[Settings]], in the mailbox window, or with `/mw friends`.
2. **Pick a player:** your guild, your party or raid, your own characters, and anyone running Mailworthy nearby. Online players come first, and their professions are shown and suggested.
3. **Pick the profession.**

That's it. From then on, everything that profession uses is mailed to them. Each binding works for **all your characters** on that realm and faction (or switch it to **this character only**). You can have up to 12.

## What a binding sends

- Every material the profession uses, the same list as in [[Profession Materials]]: cloth for a tailor, dust, essences and shards for an enchanter...
- An **Enchanting** binding also takes your **green and blue weapons and armor**, for them to disenchant.
- It sends these **whatever they're worth**. It's a gift, not a sale, so they don't count towards the "gain" total.

## What a binding doesn't send

- **Locked, soulbound and quest items, and tools.** A binding never overrides those.
- **Anything at or above its price cap.** Each binding has a cap per item: **5g each** by default, adjustable from 50s to 100g, or no cap. When the market suddenly pays a fortune for something, it goes to your bank alt to be sold instead, and the scan says so.
- **Materials your friend has levelled past.** If Bob's tailoring is past Linen Cloth, your linen goes to your bank alt (or to another binding that still needs it). Mailworthy learns Bob's skill from:
  - Bob's own Mailworthy, if they run it (see [[Mailworthy Talks to Mailworthy]]);
  - the guild roster, which shows the two main professions (not First Aid or Cooking);
  - your own character's skill, if the binding is to one of your alts.

  If nothing is known, the binding sends everything, as before.

## Skip once and Remove

In the mailbox window each binding has **Skip once** (not this visit; it comes back next time) and **Remove**. In the Friends window you can also change each binding's price cap and whether it's for all your characters.

## The right character gets it

If your friend runs Mailworthy, their addon tells yours which of **their** characters has which profession. So a binding to "Bob" for Enchanting reaches **Bob's enchanter**: Bob if they enchant, otherwise their alt that does. It never goes to their bank alt. The window always shows exactly who will get the mail ("Bob Alt — Enchanting materials (their crafter)").

## Your guild's template

Your guild master (or an officer) can hand out bindings for the whole guild: see [[Guild Template]]. Your own bindings always come first.

## Several people in one go

With bindings, one press of Send can mail several people. The top of the window lists every recipient with what they get and how many mails. The **Mailing** list is grouped by recipient, and the Send button names them ("Send to Roknua, Bob").
