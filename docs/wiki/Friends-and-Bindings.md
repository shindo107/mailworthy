# Friends and Bindings

Have a guildmate who enchants? A friend levelling tailoring? A **binding** sends a profession's materials to them every time you mail, without you thinking about it.

*"Send my Enchanting materials to Bob."* That's one binding.

## Making one

1. Open **Send materials to friends**: **Friends...** in [[Settings]], **Friends...** on the Bindings heading of the mailbox window, or `/mw friends`.
2. **Who should get your materials?** Pick from your guild, your party or raid, your own characters, and anyone running Mailworthy nearby. Online players come first, and their professions are shown and suggested. A player with the Mailworthy icon after their name runs Mailworthy too.
3. **What should Bob get?** Pick the profession, for example **Enchanting (dust, essences, shards, green and blue gear, recipes)**.

That's it. Each binding works for **all your characters** on that realm and faction (or switch it to **This character**). You can have up to 12.

## What a binding sends

- The profession's **primary materials**:

  | Profession | Gets |
  |---|---|
  | Tailoring, First Aid | cloth |
  | Leatherworking | leather and hides |
  | Blacksmithing | metal and stone |
  | Engineering | metal and stone, parts, explosives, devices |
  | Alchemy | herbs |
  | Enchanting | dust, essences and shards, plus your **green and blue weapons and armor** to disenchant |
  | Cooking | meat and fish |

- That profession's **recipes** (plans, patterns, schematics, formulas...).
- It sends these **whatever they're worth**, even with no auction price yet. It's a gift, not a sale, so they don't count towards the "gain" total.

Only primary materials: an engineer doesn't get your Linen Cloth, a blacksmith doesn't get your gems. Those follow the normal rules. Your friend can always ask for them.

**Two bindings want the same thing?** Blacksmithing and Engineering both take metal and stone. It goes to whichever friend has the **lower skill**, so both keep levelling. Your own character's bindings come before your account's, and both come before your guild's rules.

## What a binding doesn't send

- **Anything that always stays:** locks, soulbound and quest items, items an active quest asks for, tools. See [[What Stays in Your Bags]].
- **Anything at or above its price cap.** Each binding has a cap per item: **5g each** by default ("to my alt at 5g each or more"), adjustable with **-** and **+** from 50s to 100g, or "no price cap". When the market suddenly pays a fortune for something, it goes to your bank alt to be sold instead. For a recipe the cap counts even if its price is old.
- **Anything whose price lookup failed.**
- **Materials your friend has levelled past.** If Bob's tailoring is past Linen Cloth, your linen goes to your bank alt (or to another binding that still needs it). Mailworthy learns Bob's skill from:
  - Bob's own Mailworthy, if they run it (see [[Mailworthy Talks to Mailworthy]]);
  - the guild roster, which shows the two main professions (not First Aid or Cooking);
  - your own character's skill, if the binding is to one of your alts.

  If nothing is known, the binding sends everything. The mailbox window shows the skill it knows: "(guild, skill 120)".

## Class items

Got a scroll or a piece of gear whose tooltip says **Classes: Mage**, and you're not a mage? Bind your mage friend to **Mage items (only a mage can use them)**. When their class is known (party, guild, your own characters), it's the first choice in step 2.

A class binding takes only what your own class can't use, with the same price cap and Skip once. Unlike a profession binding it follows the price rules: an item without a current auction price goes to your bank alt instead (and without Auctionator or TSM, only white items go).

## Skip once and Remove

In the mailbox window each binding has **Skip once** (not this visit; **Undo skip** brings it back, and it resets when you close the mailbox) and **Remove**. In the Friends window you can also change each binding's price cap and whether it's for **All my characters** or **This character**.

## The right character gets it

If your friend runs Mailworthy, their addon tells yours which of **their** characters has which profession. So a binding to "Bob" for Enchanting reaches **Bob's enchanter**: Bob if they enchant, otherwise their alt that does. It never goes to their bank alt. The window always shows exactly who will get the mail ("Bob Alt — Enchanting materials (their crafter)").

## What you owe them

If a friend mails you more than you've mailed back, their binding in Friends says so: "(you've received 10s more from them)". See [[Mail Ledger]]; **Mail ledger...** opens it from the Friends window.

## Your guild's rules

Your guild master (or an officer) can hand out bindings for the whole guild: see [[Guild Template]]. Your own bindings always come first.

## Several people in one go

With bindings, one press of Send can mail several people. The top of the window lists every recipient with what they get and how many mails. The **Mailing** list is grouped by recipient, and the Send button names them ("Send to Roknua, Bob"). Binding mail arrives as "Mailworthy 1/1: Enchanting materials".
