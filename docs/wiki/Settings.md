# Settings

Open **Mailworthy settings** with **Settings** in the mailbox window, `/mw`, or the minimap button. It works away from a mailbox too, with mouse or controller. Every on/off button is lit while it's on.

## Bank alt

**Bank alt: Roknua — Change...** opens **Who gets your mail?**: your characters on this realm and faction, **This character is my bank alt**, or type a name (see [[Getting Started]]). **Forget** removes an old name from the list, **Back** returns to the settings.

No bank alt yet? The row reads *None: what's worth selling stays in your bags.* and the button is **Choose...**. A bank alt is optional: see [[Your Bank Alt]].

## What gets mailed

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Gain at least** (- / +) | 1s | An item has to make at least this much more than vendoring to be mailed |
| **and at least N% over the vendor price** (- / +) | 25% | ...and at least this much more, proportionally (so a 1g vendor item needs 25s more) |
| **Auction listing** 2h / 8h / 24h | 24h | The listing length you use; it sets the deposit counted |
| **Include disenchant value** | on with a disenchant addon | Compare disenchanting for green, blue and purple gear. The button names the source: Siz DisenchantValue, Disenchantry or era tables (see [[Price Sources]]) |
| **Keep my professions' materials** | on | Keep what your professions use until you've levelled past it; the button lists your professions and skills. Turn off to send everything to your bank alt. See [[Profession Materials]] |
| **Sell what I gather** | off | For gatherer-crafters: mail what Mining, Herbalism, Skinning or Fishing brings in, instead of keeping it for the craft |

## In your bags

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Bag tags** | bank alt only | **every character**: show the AH / B / DE / V tags on all your characters |
| **Lock glow** | in Mailworthy | **always**: show the blue lock glow all the time, not just while the Mailworthy window is open |

## More

- **Friends...** (or **Friends (2 bindings)...**): your bindings. See [[Friends and Bindings]].
- **Bank alt summary...**: what to list, disenchant or vendor. See [[Your Bank Alt]].
- **Guild...**: your guild's template. It reads **Guild (new template)...**, **Guild (rules on)...** or **Guild (rules off)...**. See [[Guild Template]].
- **Mail ledger...**: the log of your mail and what you owe. See [[Mail Ledger]].
- **Minimap button: shown / hidden**.

The line at the bottom says where prices come from, for example "Prices: Auctionator". See [[Price Sources]].
